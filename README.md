## How to Explore This Portfolio

1. **Start with the case study** that matches the type of work you care about (AI-enabled delivery, enterprise governance, or end-to-end product strategy).
2. **Review the practical steps** in the table above to understand the process followed.
3. **Open the directory** and examine the artefacts (documents, diagrams, frameworks, prototypes).
4. **Look for decision rationale** — each artefact is intended to show not just the output, but the thinking behind prioritisation, trade-offs, and stakeholder alignment.

## Sample Product Highlight: Textopedia

The `03-b2b-saas-strategy` directory contains a worked example of building a new product concept (Textopedia — a global textbook discovery and community platform).  

**Practical steps executed:**
- Market and competitive gap analysis
- Product vision and business case
- Primary and secondary user personas with supporting research synthesis
- Agile roadmap (Now / Next / Someday) with goals, candidates, and key results
- Key insight visualisation
- High-fidelity UI prototypes aligned to a primary persona journey

These artefacts demonstrate end-to-end product leadership from problem framing through to tangible design and prioritisation decisions.
