Textopedia — Sample Product Case Study

A global textbook discovery and community platform.

Sample product idea demonstrating end-to-end product leadership: problem framing, vision, personas, roadmap, and prioritisation.

Practical Steps





Problem discovery — Validated the gap between fragmented textbook sourcing (syllabus → Amazon/Chegg → peer chat) and the need for cost, edition, and peer-clarity signals.



Vision & positioning — Defined Textopedia as the social + access layer for textbooks (Goodreads-style community with buy/rent/open paths and author engagement).



Persona development — Created primary (budget student) and secondary (author/faculty) personas with supporting insight synthesis.



Roadmap & prioritisation — Built an outcome-oriented Now / Next / Someday roadmap with goals, candidates, and key results.



Prototype & validation — Produced high-fidelity UI flows aligned to the primary persona journey.

What This Demonstrates





Translating a broad idea into a focused, testable product concept.



Balancing student value (cost, edition confidence, peer insight) with author value (feedback and engagement).



Prioritising an MVP that proves discovery + community + access-link interest before expanding.



Using personas and structured roadmapping to guide scope and sequencing decisions.
