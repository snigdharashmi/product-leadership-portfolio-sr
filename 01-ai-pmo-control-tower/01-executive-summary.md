# AI PMO Control Tower — Executive Summary

**Version:** 1.0 (Portfolio)  
**Deployment model:** Hybrid Azure + On-premises  
**Product type:** Internal enterprise platform  
**Portfolio:** 6 clients / 2 delivery models  
**Initial delivery:** 90-day pilot MVP

## Problem

The PMO currently operates in a reactive mode. Information is fragmented across Jira, Confluence, Harvest, Outlook, Teams, and delivery infrastructure. Status reporting consumes significant effort, and risks or readiness issues are often discovered late.

## Solution

A centralized multi-agent intelligence platform that consolidates operational data and provides specialist AI agents to support Product Managers, Project Managers, Release Managers, and leadership. The platform moves the organisation toward proactive, evidence-based delivery management while keeping humans in control of scope, budget, and production decisions.

## Six Strategic Outcomes

1. Create a unified view of all client engagements, risks, releases, budgets, and milestones.
2. Assist Product Managers with backlog analysis, requirements, prioritisation, and roadmap decisions.
3. Assist Project Managers with planning, dependency management, effort forecasting, and delivery coordination.
4. Automate preparation of monthly maintenance releases, readiness assessments, and release documentation.
5. Identify emerging delivery risks using Jira history, project discussions, Harvest effort, and technical signals.
6. Execute approved administrative workflows while retaining human authority over scope, budgets, commitments, and production releases.

## In Scope vs Out of Scope (MVP)

| In scope for initial platform | Out of scope for MVP |
|-------------------------------|----------------------|
| Six internal client workspaces | Commercial SaaS tenancy and billing |
| PM, Product, and Release copilots | Autonomous production deployment |
| Cross-source project intelligence | Replacing Jira or Harvest |
| Support and implementation workflows | Fully automated contractual decisions |
| Forecasting and risk alerts | Unsupervised resource reassignment |
| Human-approved workflow updates | Ingestion of unrestricted private messages |

## Pilot Intent

Deliver selected vertical slices that prove value on one support client and one implementation client within 90 days, rather than attempting full production depth across all capabilities.
